Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one editorial case-study thumbnail for a Japanese technology company's existing website. This is conceptual artwork, not a customer screenshot or a real client product. Produce ONE standalone landscape image, not a contact sheet.

Art direction: a sophisticated physical still-life photographed for a contemporary design magazine. Tangible sculptural objects in matte porcelain, uncoated ivory paper, satin aluminum and a restrained cobalt-blue accent (#0553dd), on a pale warm-gray seamless studio surface. Photoreal material texture, precise edges, subtle imperfect paper grain, believable contact shadows and restrained reflections. Quiet, architectural, minimal and premium. Off-white, gray, silver and blue only. Avoid the toy-like look of glossy stock 3D icons. No floating objects or cartoon style. No generic neon gradients, glowing blobs, circuit-board backgrounds, robots, brains or decorative sparkles.

Composition: landscape around 16:10, camera at a refined three-quarter product-photography angle, with a close, well-composed hero object and breathing room. Keep all essential objects in the central 70 percent so the image works cropped to a small horizontal website card or a wider article banner. Subject should be prominent and legible at thumbnail size, not a tiny isolated icon. Soft directional daylight from upper left, gentle sculptural shadows, balanced white exposure, crisp tactile detail. The background should blend naturally with an off-white corporate website. No webpage layout, infographic panels or fake dashboard.

Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, labels, captions, watermark, actual screen interfaces, customer data, recognizable bank-card branding, people or hands. No visually busy collection of symbols. Use at most one main sculptural arrangement and one or two supporting forms. Output the highest available native resolution with no artificial upscaling.

Subject: recurring subscription payments and an evolving software platform. Create a carefully art-directed still-life of a single unbranded cobalt-blue satin-metal payment card standing at a slight angle, embraced by an elegant continuous ivory ceramic loop resting on the surface. Two small ivory modular blocks sit on the loop's base, suggesting account seats. The continuous loop conveys recurring billing; the blank blue card is the visual focus. No money, coins, currency signs or card digits. The objects must feel like a beautifully fabricated physical design installation, with close attention to proportions and grounded shadows.
